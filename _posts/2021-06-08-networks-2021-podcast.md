Alex Abbey's and Yanir Marmor's work on disease epidemiology over real-life interaction networks is accepted as an oral presentation at [Networks 2021](https://networks2021.net/).

Alex and Yanir discuss the work in the [latest episode](https://en.snapod.net/podcast/episode/aaec1bd4/netfrix-ep17) of the NetFrix podcast, and talk about the effect of changing temporal network density on community transmission.
