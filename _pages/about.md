My research sits where people, AI, and networks meet. Four threads run through it, and each one feeds the others.

**Human–AI teaming.** As generative AI moves into creative and professional work, I study what changes when people and machines think together: how groups generate ideas with a language model in the room, how politeness and trust shift when the partner is a machine, and how professionals defend their occupational identity as AI encroaches on their craft. This work appears at CHI, CSCW, IUI, and ICIS, and I chair the ACM IUI Steering Committee.

**Temporal networks.** Real interactions have rhythm. People meet at particular moments, for particular durations, and my group showed that these dynamics govern how an airborne disease moves through a community more strongly than the transmissibility of the pathogen itself. I gave the keynote on this work at NetSciX'25, and presented it more recently at SigmaPhi. We released the first open-source temporal path-preserving random network generator alongside it.

**Learning via Surprisability.** Most machine learning hunts for the patterns that show up in data. I also ask the opposite question: which *expected* signals are absent, and what does their absence reveal? Learning via Surprisability builds on the insight that a missing feature can be as informative as a present one. It attributes authorship, flags impersonation, and surfaces interpretable anomalies in high-dimensional data, without labels.

**Computational human behavior.** I model what people reveal through what they write. We showed that the emotions a viewer felt while watching a film can be recovered from the review they wrote afterwards, and used those emotional signatures to diversify recommendations and to explain what makes a review useful to other readers.

I chair the [ACM IUI Steering Committee](https://iui.acm.org/steering/) and serve as an Associate Editor at *ACM Transactions on Interactive Intelligent Systems*. I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, and an M.Sc. and B.Sc. from the Technion. Prospective students: please see the [lab page](/people/) and get in touch.
